**Zomato Food Delivery Dashboard**

I'm excited to share my latest Excel Zomato Food Delivery Dashboard , designed for real time tracking and analysis..

📌**Project Overview**
This project is an interactive Zomato Food Delivery Dashboard created in Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides insights into food orders, revenue, customer ratings, delivery performance, restaurant performance, payment methods, and food categories.

## Dataset used:

<a href="https://github.com/Aniketyadav004/Excel-/blob/main/ZomatoProject%20(Aniket%20yadav).xlsx">Dataset view</a>

🎯**Objectives**
Analyze food delivery order trends,
Monitor revenue and overall business performance,
Evaluate restaurant-wise and city-wise performance,
Understand customer preferences through food category analysis,
Track delivery efficiency and customer ratings,
Visualize payment method usage and order status distribution.


📂**Dataset Information**
The dataset contains 1000 food delivery orders with the following fields:
Order ID, Order Date, City, Restaurant, Food Category, Quantity, Revenue, Rating, Payment Method, Delivery Time (Minutes),
Order Status.

📋 **Key Insights at glance**
City-wise Orders,
Pune recorded the highest number of orders,
Mumbai and Lucknow also contributed significantly to total orders,
Restaurant Performance,
Barbeque Nation and Behrouz Biryani received the highest order counts,
Restaurant ratings help identify customer satisfaction trends,
Payment Preferences,
UPI emerged as the most preferred payment method,
Digital payments dominated over cash transactions,
Food Category Trends,
Chinese cuisine received the highest number of orders,
Burger, Biryani, Pizza, and South Indian foods maintained strong demand,
